Biography

Mtre Nicolas Melillo practises mainly in civil litigation, with a particular focus on construction law. He advises and represents contractors, construction professionals, real estate developers, manufacturers, real estate brokers, property owners and businesses. His practice also includes residential lease law, commercial law and condominium law.

He handles a wide range of matters, including latent defects, design and construction defects and loss of the work. He also takes on hypothecary recourses based on legal construction hypothecs, actions on account and claims for damages. He represents his clients in applications for injunctions and safeguard orders.

Attentive to his clients’ needs, Mtre Melillo supports them at every stage of a file. He drafts legal opinions, reviews contracts, advises them on pre-litigation steps and represents them before the courts. He also acts in arbitrations, mediations and settlement conferences. He makes a point of giving them a straight answer about their options and chances of success so that they can make informed decisions.

Mtre Melillo pleads before the Superior Court and the Court of Québec, and has also pleaded before the Québec Court of Appeal. His approach is pragmatic, strategic and results-oriented. An athlete driven by a competitive spirit, he handles his files with efficiency, determination and rigour.

He graduated in law from the Université de Sherbrooke in 2016 and completed the École du Barreau du Québec in 2017. He articled at a firm specializing in construction law before being sworn in in April 2018. He then practised at mid-size and large firms.

Biography

Mtre Étienne Paradis practises mainly in civil and commercial litigation as well as business law.

He advises and represents a diverse clientele, including businesses, developers, general and specialized contractors, property owners and individuals.

His litigation practice focuses particularly on construction law and real estate law. He handles construction claims, construction defects, legal hypothecs, commercial leases and latent defects. He also represents his clients in various contractual disputes and injunction proceedings, before the civil courts and arbitral bodies.

In business law, his practice includes shareholder disputes and oppression remedies, the incorporation and reorganization of companies, the drafting and negotiation of shareholder agreements, and the purchase and sale of businesses.

His approach is based on practical and strategic advice tailored to the legal and business realities of each file, both in managing disputes and in preventing disputes and risks.

He graduated in law from the Université de Sherbrooke in 2016 and was admitted to the Barreau du Québec in 2017. He began his career at a firm specializing in civil and commercial litigation, where he developed his practice in construction law and business law.
